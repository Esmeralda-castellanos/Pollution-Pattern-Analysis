6. Environmental-justice / community advocacy group
7. Academic peer reviewer (capstone context)
8. Course instructor / evaluator

## Power/Interest Priorities
- **High power, high interest (manage closely):** RIVM data team, municipal environmental policy
- **High interest, lower power (keep informed, invite feedback):** GGD, community advocacy groups
- **High power, lower interest (keep satisfied):** provincial environmental service, urban planning
- **Lower power, lower interest:** general public / broader academic audience

Top 3 priority relationships for this project: RIVM data team (data integrity), municipal policy
officer (actionability), community advocacy group (equity check on interpretation).

## Communication Plan by Phase
- **Problem framing (now):** this Project Proposal, shared for early sanity-check on scope and
  relevance.
- **Analysis in progress:** interim notebook sections (EDA, preliminary clustering) available for
  review; explicitly flag any surprising or counter-intuitive patterns for feedback.
- **Results ready:** final notebook with an impact-reporting section, written for a mixed
  technical/non-technical audience.
- **Implementation support:** document recommended next steps (Section 10 of the notebook) rather
  than assume clusters will be acted on without further validation.

## Trust-Building Mechanisms
1. Explicit "limitations" section documenting sparse coverage, single-year scope, and no
   causal/source claims.
2. Plain-language explanation of what clustering can and cannot tell us, alongside the technical
   metrics.
3. Open documentation of all cleaning and feature-engineering decisions, so results can be checked
   or reproduced.
